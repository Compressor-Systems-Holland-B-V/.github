# Compressor Systems Holland B.V.

**Tailor-made compressor packages for a more sustainable industry.**

Compressor Systems Holland (CSH), a Fischcon company based in Vianen, the Netherlands, has spent more than 40 years designing and building compact, high-quality compressor packages for the inland shipping, on/offshore, and energy sectors. We work in the midrange of compressor pressure, from 0.1 to 80 bar with gas flows up to 3,000 m³/h, and support applications such as hydrogen, bio-LNG, and ammonia.
